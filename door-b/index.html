Atmos Rewards · Gold status

I have Atmos Gold and I’m flying Qatar Airways using Atmos points. What does that get me?

  • Award earning
  • Status mapping
  • Airport benefits
  • Lounge access

More than an award seat. The trip still earns Atmos status points, while Atmos Gold maps to oneworld Sapphire—giving you priority check-in, boarding and baggage handling, plus access for you and one guest to Qatar Airways’ Gold Lounge South and Platinum & Gold Lounge North in Doha, even in Economy. Add your Atmos number to the booking so your benefits are recognized.

I use Atmos as my main program. Can I use my points for a Japan Airlines trip from Seattle to Tokyo—and still make progress toward status?

Yes. When Japan Airlines award space is available, you can book the Tokyo trip with Atmos points. The award still earns Atmos status points, and any eligible oneworld benefits attached to your Atmos status travel with you on JAL.
